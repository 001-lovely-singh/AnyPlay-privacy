Privacy Policy for Any Play

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Any Play ("the app") lets you play RPG Maker MV/MZ games stored on your own device. This page explains what information the app and its third-party services collect, and how it's used.

Information collected by advertising (Google AdMob)

Any Play shows ads through Google AdMob to support the app, since there's no paid or subscription version. AdMob may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier
  • General device information (device model, operating system version, language, general location such as country/region)
  • App usage information related to ad requests and interactions

This data is collected and processed by Google, not stored or seen by the app's developer directly. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from AdMob for full details.

Your game library

Any Play needs broad storage access ("All files access") to find, read, and write RPG Maker game folders you already have on your device — a regular per-folder permission isn't enough for a general file browser and player like this one.

Your library (game titles, file locations, and your compatibility settings for each game) is stored only in a local database on your own device. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or made accessible to the developer or any third party. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Game save data is stored on your device, in the game's own save folder, the same way a desktop copy of the game would keep it. Any Play does not collect or transmit save data.

Games packed inside a single file

Some games are distributed as one large .exe or package.nw file instead of a normal folder. When you add such a game, Any Play extracts the game's files from that file into the same folder on your device so they can be played.

  • Extraction happens entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded or sent over the internet.
  • Files already in your folder (for example your own save files) are never overwritten.
  • After a successful extraction, the app asks whether you want to delete the original packed file to save space. It is deleted only if you agree, and only after the app has checked that the extracted game is complete. You can choose to keep it.

Notifications and background activity

Extracting a large game can take several minutes. To keep it running when your screen turns off or you switch to another app, Any Play runs the extraction as a foreground service and shows a notification with the progress and a Cancel button. It also keeps the device awake only while the extraction is running.

On Android 13 and later the app asks for permission to show notifications. This permission is optional: if you decline, extraction still works, you just won't see the progress notification. Notifications are created and shown on your device only; the app does not collect or send any information through them.

What this app does not do

  • No account or sign-in is required or offered.
  • No personal information (name, email, phone number) is collected by the app itself.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK is included beyond what AdMob itself uses for ad delivery.
  • Game files never leave your device — they're served only to the app's own in-app browser component over a local, device-only connection, never over the internet.

Children's privacy

Any Play is not directed at children, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, contact the developer using the details below so it can be addressed.

Your choices

You can opt out of personalized advertising, or reset your advertising identifier, from your device's own settings (typically under Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or the services it uses change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
