# 🎮 AnyPlay

Play your favorite RPG Maker MV/MZ games right on your Android phone or tablet. No PC needed.

AnyPlay lets you import a game folder straight from your device and play it with touch controls, a controller, or a keyboard.

## 📦 Technologies

- `Android`
- `RPG Maker MV/MZ support`
- `Kotlin`
- `WebView`

## 🦄 Features

Here's what you can do with AnyPlay:

- Import Games :
Import any RPG Maker MV/MZ game straight from your device.

- Touch Controls :
Full touch controls with an on-screen gamepad, now fully customizable (move, resize, recolor).

- Controller & Keyboard Support :
Play with a physical controller or keyboard.

- Auto Save Management :
Pick up right where you left off.

- Free & Offline :
100% free, and works fully offline once your games are imported.

## 🎯 How It Works

1. Download the APK from the link below
2. If prompted, allow installs from unknown sources
3. Open AnyPlay and tap "Add a new game" or "Unpack a game"
4. Pick your RPG Maker folder and start playing
